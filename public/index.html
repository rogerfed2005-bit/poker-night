Rules

Joining and seats

  • The host starts a game and shares the game code or invite link (tap CODE at the top right). Friends open the link, type a nickname and join.
  • Everyone joins as a watcher. Tap an empty SIT seat to play — the host approves the request (the host can switch approval off). Up to 8 seats.
  • Sitting down buys you in for the table's buy-in (200 chips by default). You're dealt in from the next hand.
  • The host presses Start game once 2 or more players are seated. After that hands deal automatically.

Each hand

  • The dealer button (D) moves one seat clockwise every hand. The next player posts the small blind (1 by default), the next the big blind (2). With 2 players the dealer posts the small blind. If the host sets an ante, everyone posts it too.
  • The small blind chooses the game for each hand — Texas, 999, 1 3 5, Theenpathi or Omaha — before cards are dealt. If they don't pick in time, Texas is played.

Betting (no-limit)

  • Four betting rounds: pre-flop, flop (3 community cards), turn (4th) and river (5th).
  • Pre-flop, the player left of the big blind acts first. After the flop, the first player still in left of the dealer acts first.
  • On your turn: Fold, Check, Call or Bet / Raise up to your whole stack. Tap RAISE, then type the amount, slide, use − / + or the MIN / ½ POT / ¾ POT / POT / ALL IN buttons.
  • A raise must be at least as big as the previous bet or raise in that round. You can always go all-in for less.
  • When it isn't your turn you can pre-select CHECK / FOLD, CHECK or CALL ANY — it plays as soon as your turn comes.
  • There is a turn timer (30 seconds by default, shown as a bar on your seat). If it runs out you check if you can, otherwise you fold.
  • All-in players can only win what they matched from each player; extra bets form a side pot.
  • If everyone else folds, the last player wins without showing (they can tap Show cards if they want).

Showdown

  • With 2+ players left after the river, everyone still in chooses which of their own hole cards they are playing — see the Choosing cards tab. The game completes your hand from the community cards and shows a preview.
  • The game never picks for you: your choice counts even if a better one existed. Press Confirm my cards within 60 seconds or your hand is mucked. (If nobody in a pot confirms, everyone's best hand is used.)
  • Hands are revealed once everyone has confirmed. Cards used are outlined green, side-game cards blue. Tap Details on the table for the full breakdown.
  • Equal hands split the pot. Odd chips go to the tied winners closest to the dealer's left.

Buy-ins, leaving and the ledger

  • Buy in (Options) adds another stack whenever you like, except in the middle of a hand you were dealt into — wait until the hand ends. If you're out of chips or away, you can buy in any time.
  • Leave seat cashes out your stack (your buy-out) and makes you a watcher again; sit down again to buy back in. If you're still in a hand, you leave when it ends.
  • Away keeps your seat but skips you until you press I'm back.
  • The Ledger shows each player's buy-ins, buy-outs, stack and net, with every buy-in and buy-out timed. Download it as a spreadsheet before you finish — it's lost if the server restarts.
  • If you lose connection, reopen the link or rejoin with the same nickname. While you're away you're checked or folded when your timer runs out.

Texas Hold'em

You choose: at showdown, tap the hole cards you are playing — 2, 1 or 0.
  • Dealt: 2 hole cards each, plus 5 community cards over the flop, turn and river.
  • Your hand: the best 5 cards from your hole cards and the board, using 2, 1 or 0 of your hole cards (0 = "playing the board").
  • Winner: the best poker hand takes the whole pot (see Hand rankings).

Example: you hold A♥ 9♥ and the board is K♥ 7♥ 3♥ 8♣ 4♠. Tap both cards → Flush, Ace high. Tap only the 9♥ → no flush, and that's what counts.

999

You choose: before the showdown, select the 3 cards for your 999 number. At showdown confirm them, then tap which of your other 2 cards play Texas (2, 1 or 0).
  • Dealt: 5 hole cards each, plus 5 community cards.
  • Split your cards: 3 cards make your 999 number and the other 2 play Texas.
  • The 999 number: cards 2–9 count as their value; 10, J, Q, K and A count as 0. Your 3 digits are arranged into the biggest number — the closer to 999 the better; the highest number wins.
  • Choosing: tap your 3 cards any time during the hand — the number shows as you pick, and you can change it until the showdown.
  • Pot split: half to the best Texas hand, half to the highest 999 number. One player can win both halves. Two players tied on 999 share that half (a quarter of the pot each), and so on. With an odd pot the extra chip goes to the Texas half.

Examples: 9 8 7 → 987. 9 9 A → 990. K 5 3 → 530. Q J 10 → 000.

Split example: pot of 100. Ravi has the best Texas hand → 50. Shri and Vroon both have 998 → 25 each.

1 3 5

You choose: at showdown, tap exactly 1, 3 or 5 of your hole cards.
  • Dealt: 5 hole cards each, plus 5 community cards.
  • Your hand: a normal 5-card hand using exactly 1, 3 or 5 of your hole cards, completed with 4, 2 or 0 community cards. Using 2 or 4 is not allowed.
  • Winner: the best poker hand takes the whole pot.

Example: you hold Q♥ Q♦ 2♣ 3♣ 4♦, board Q♠ 9♣ 9♦ 7♥ 8♠.

  • Full house Q Q Q 9 9 would use 2 of your cards → not allowed.
  • 1 card: Q♥ + Q♠ 9♣ 9♦ 8♠ → Two Pair.
  • 3 cards: Q♥ Q♦ 4♦ + Q♠ 9♣ → Three Queens — the best legal hand.
  • 5 cards: Q♥ Q♦ 2♣ 3♣ 4♦ → Pair of Queens.

Theenpathi

You choose: before the showdown, select the 3 cards you want for your Theenpathi hand. At showdown confirm them, then tap which of your other 2 cards play Texas (2, 1 or 0).
  • Dealt: 5 hole cards each, plus 5 community cards.
  • Split your cards: 3 cards make your Theenpathi hand (the board isn't used for it) and the other 2 play Texas.
  • Choosing: tap 3 cards during the hand — the hand name shows as you pick (e.g. "Pair of Kings"). You can change it until the showdown.
  • Pot split: half to the best Texas hand, half to the best Theenpathi hand; ties share their half, just like 999.

Theenpathi hands, best to worst

  1. Trail — three of a kind, e.g. 7 7 7 (A A A is the best).
  2. Straight flush — 3 in a row, same suit, e.g. 4♠ 5♠ 6♠.
  3. Straight — 3 in a row, e.g. 9 10 J.
  4. Flush — 3 of the same suit, e.g. K♥ 9♥ 2♥.
  5. Pair — e.g. Q Q 5.
  6. High card — anything else.
  • Straights: A-K-Q is the highest, then K-Q-J … 4-3-2, and A-2-3 is the lowest.
  • Same type: flush and high card compare highest card first, then the next. Pairs compare the pair, then the odd card. Exactly equal hands share.

Omaha

You choose: at showdown, tap exactly 2 of your 4 hole cards.
  • Dealt: 4 hole cards each, plus 5 community cards.
  • Your hand: exactly 2 of your hole cards plus exactly 3 community cards.
  • Winner: the best poker hand takes the whole pot.

Watch out: A♥ K♥ Q♥ J♥ with one heart on the board is not a flush — only 2 of your hearts count, so the board needs 3 hearts.

Poker hands, best to worst

For Texas, 1 3 5, Omaha and the Texas half of 999 / Theenpathi. Aces are high; A-2-3-4-5 is the lowest straight.

  1. Straight flush — 5 in a row, same suit (A K Q J 10 = Royal Flush).
  2. Four of a kind — e.g. 9 9 9 9.
  3. Full house — three of a kind + a pair.
  4. Flush — 5 of the same suit.
  5. Straight — 5 in a row.
  6. Three of a kind
  7. Two pair
  8. Pair
  9. High card

Same type: higher cards win, then kickers. Suits never matter. Theenpathi's 3-card ranking is on its own tab.

Options menu (top left)

  • Buy in, Away / I'm back, Leave seat and Show cards (after a hand).
  • Preferences (just for you): sounds, four-colour deck (♦ blue, ♣ green), amounts in big blinds.
  • Log / Ledger (bottom left): every action of every hand, and the money ledger.
  • Chat and reactions (😀) — reactions pop up over your seat.

Host controls

  • Start, Pause / Resume (pausing stops all timers and actions).
  • Game settings: small blind, big blind, ante, buy-in amount, turn time, showdown time and seat approval. Changes made during a hand apply from the next hand.
  • Players: accept or decline seat requests, make someone else host, or remove a player. A removed player folds, their chips are cashed out in the ledger, and they can't rejoin under that name.
