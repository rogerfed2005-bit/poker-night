Rules

The table

  • Up to 8 players. Everyone starts with 200 chips. Blinds are 1 (small) and 2 (big).
  • The dealer button moves one seat to the left every hand. The player left of the dealer posts the small blind, the next player the big blind. With only 2 players, the dealer posts the small blind.
  • The small blind chooses the game for each hand — Texas, 999, 1 3 5, Theenpathi or Omaha — before any cards are dealt. Blinds are then posted and cards dealt.

Betting (no-limit)

  • Four betting rounds: pre-flop (after hole cards), flop (3 community cards), turn (4th card) and river (5th card).
  • Pre-flop, the player left of the big blind acts first (with 2 players, the dealer/small blind). After the flop, the first player still in left of the dealer acts first.
  • On your turn: Fold, Check (if nothing to call), Call, or Bet / Raise to any amount up to your whole stack. Type the amount, use the slider or the Min / ½ Pot / Pot / All-in buttons.
  • The smallest bet is 2. A raise must be at least as big as the previous bet or raise in that round. You can always go all-in for less.
  • If you go all-in, you can only win what you matched from each other player; the rest goes into a side pot for the players who keep betting.
  • If everyone else folds, the last player wins the pot without showing.

Showdown — you choose your cards

  • When betting ends with 2 or more players left, all 5 community cards are dealt (even after an early all-in) and every player still in picks which of their own hole cards they are playing. The game completes your 5-card hand with the best community cards and shows a preview.
  • How many hole cards you may use depends on the game (see each tab). In 999 and Theenpathi you also confirm your 3 side-game cards.
  • The game never picks for you: your choice counts even if a better one existed.
  • You have 60 seconds to press Confirm. If you don't, your hand is mucked and can't win. (If nobody in a pot confirms, everyone's best hand is used so the pot isn't stuck.)
  • Hands are revealed only when everyone has confirmed. The showdown panel shows each player's hole cards used (green), side-game cards (blue), the community cards that completed the hand, and what they won.

Ties and odd chips

  • Equal hands split the pot (suits never break a tie). If chips don't divide evenly, the extra chips go to the tied winners closest to the dealer's left.

Chips, buy-ins and the ledger

  • Buy in adds 200 chips at any time. If you're in a hand, they're added when it ends. Every buy-in is counted.
  • Cash out takes your stack off the table (your buy-out) and sits you out; buy in again to come back.
  • Ledger shows each player's buy-ins, buy-outs, stack and net result, and downloads as a spreadsheet. It resets if the server restarts, so download it at the end of the night.
  • If you lose connection, just reopen the link or rejoin with the same name. While you're away, you're checked or folded after 20 seconds on your turn.
  • The host can remove a player by tapping their seat. A removed player folds any hand they're in, their remaining chips are cashed out in the ledger, and they can't rejoin that game.

Texas Hold'em

  • Dealt: 2 hole cards each, plus 5 community cards over the flop, turn and river.
  • Your hand: the best 5 cards from your hole cards and the board. You may use 2, 1 or 0 of your hole cards (0 = "playing the board").
  • At showdown: tap the hole cards you are using; the game fills in the best board cards.
  • Winner: the best poker hand takes the whole pot (see Hand rankings).

Example: you hold A♥ 9♥ and the board is K♥ 7♥ 3♥ 8♣ 4♠. Using both cards gives a Flush, Ace high (A♥ 9♥ K♥ 7♥ 3♥). Using only the 9♥ gives no flush — and that is what would count if you chose it.

999

  • Dealt: 5 hole cards each, plus the usual 5 community cards.
  • Split your cards: 3 cards make your 999 number and the other 2 play Texas.
  • The 999 number: cards 2–9 count as their value; 10, J, Q, K and A count as 0. Arrange your 3 digits to make the biggest number — the closer to 999, the better. The highest number wins.
  • The Texas part: your other 2 cards play Texas with the board — you may use 2, 1 or 0 of them.
  • Choosing: tap your 3 cards for 999 any time during the hand — it's private and you can change it. At showdown it becomes final: Step 1 confirms your 999 cards, Step 2 picks which of the other 2 you use for Texas.
  • Pot split: half the pot goes to the best Texas hand and half to the highest 999 number. One player can win both halves. If 2 players tie for the best 999 number they share that half (a quarter of the pot each), and the same for 3 or more. Ties in the Texas half split the same way. With an odd pot, the extra chip goes to the Texas half.

Examples: 9 8 7 → 987. 9 9 A → 990 (A is 0). K 5 3 → 530. Q J 10 → 000.

Split example: pot of 100. Ravi has the best Texas hand → wins 50. Shri and Vroon both have 998 → 25 each.

1 3 5

  • Dealt: 5 hole cards each, plus 5 community cards.
  • Your hand: a normal 5-card poker hand that uses exactly 1, 3 or 5 of your hole cards — completed with 4, 2 or 0 community cards. Using 2 or 4 of your cards is not allowed.
  • At showdown: tap 1, 3 or 5 of your cards; the game adds the best board cards. Confirm only works with a legal number of cards.
  • Winner: the best poker hand takes the whole pot.

Example: you hold Q♥ Q♦ 2♣ 3♣ 4♦ and the board is Q♠ 9♣ 9♦ 7♥ 8♠.

  • Full house Q Q Q 9 9 would use Q♥ Q♦ = 2 of your cards → not allowed.
  • 1 card: Q♥ + Q♠ 9♣ 9♦ 8♠ → Two Pair.
  • 3 cards: Q♥ Q♦ 4♦ + Q♠ 9♣ → Three Queens — the best legal hand.
  • 5 cards: Q♥ Q♦ 2♣ 3♣ 4♦ → Pair of Queens.

Theenpathi

  • Dealt: 5 hole cards each, plus 5 community cards.
  • Split your cards: 3 cards make your Theenpathi hand and the other 2 play Texas. The 3 Theenpathi cards have nothing to do with the board.
  • Choosing: tap your 3 Theenpathi cards before the showdown — the hand name shows as you pick (e.g. "Pair of Kings"). It's private and you can change it until the showdown, where Step 1 confirms it and Step 2 picks which of your other 2 cards you use for Texas (2, 1 or 0).
  • Pot split: half to the best Texas hand, half to the best Theenpathi hand; ties share their half (just like 999).

Theenpathi hands, best to worst

  1. Trail — three of a kind, e.g. 7 7 7. Higher rank wins (A A A is the best hand).
  2. Straight flush — 3 in a row, same suit, e.g. 4♠ 5♠ 6♠.
  3. Straight — 3 in a row, mixed suits, e.g. 9 10 J.
  4. Flush — 3 of the same suit, e.g. K♥ 9♥ 2♥.
  5. Pair — e.g. Q Q 5.
  6. High card — anything else.
  • Straights: A-K-Q is the highest, then K-Q-J … down to 4-3-2, and A-2-3 is the lowest. A higher top card wins.
  • Same type of hand: flush and high card compare the highest card, then the next, then the last. Pairs compare the pair, then the odd card.
  • Exactly equal hands share (suits don't matter).

Omaha

  • Dealt: 4 hole cards each, plus 5 community cards.
  • Your hand: exactly 2 of your 4 hole cards plus exactly 3 community cards — no more, no less.
  • At showdown: tap exactly 2 cards; the game adds the best 3 board cards.
  • Winner: the best poker hand takes the whole pot.

Watch out: holding A♥ K♥ Q♥ J♥ with only one heart on the board is not a flush — you can use only 2 of your hearts, so you'd need 3 hearts on the board.

Poker hands, best to worst

Used for Texas, 1 3 5, Omaha and the Texas half of 999 / Theenpathi. Aces are high, but A-2-3-4-5 also counts as the lowest straight.

  1. Straight flush — 5 in a row, same suit (A K Q J 10 of one suit is a Royal Flush).
  2. Four of a kind — e.g. 9 9 9 9 + any.
  3. Full house — three of a kind + a pair, e.g. K K K 4 4.
  4. Flush — 5 of the same suit.
  5. Straight — 5 in a row, e.g. 6 7 8 9 10.
  6. Three of a kind — e.g. 7 7 7 + two others.
  7. Two pair — e.g. J J 4 4 + one.
  8. Pair — e.g. A A + three others.
  9. High card — none of the above; highest card wins.

Same type of hand: the higher cards win (for two pair, the top pair first; for a full house, the three of a kind first). Leftover cards ("kickers") break ties. Suits never matter.

Theenpathi's 3-card ranking is on the Theenpathi tab.
